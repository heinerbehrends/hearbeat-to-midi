Connect the device

Plug in the device, press Connect and choose its serial port:

  • DevKit (two USB-C ports): the UART port with the CH343 chip, VID:PID 1A86:55D3 (often listed as "USB Single Serial").
  • XIAO ESP32-S3: its only USB port.

Only one program can use the port: close the PlatformIO serial monitor first. Or press Try without a device to explore the page with a simulated device.

Restore all defaults?

Every setting goes back to its default from include/Settings.h and applies at once.

It is not permanent until saved: a restart brings the saved values back. Choose Restore and save to make the defaults permanent.
